Privacy Policy

Applies to Quick Unit Converter, Focus Timer – Pomodoro and Text Toolbox. Last updated: 4 November 2026.

The apps do not collect, send or sell personal data. They have no accounts, analytics or advertising trackers. Your settings, text, history and statistics are stored only on your device (browser local storage) and you can delete them by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

If you buy the Pro upgrade in the Microsoft Store version, the purchase is processed by Microsoft. The app only asks the Store whether Pro is owned; it does not receive your payment details.

Contact: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.

Politica de confidențialitate

Aplicațiile nu colectează, nu trimit și nu vând date personale. Nu au conturi, statistici de utilizare sau trackere de reclame. Setările, textul, istoricul și statisticile rămân doar pe dispozitivul tău. Achiziția Pro din Microsoft Store este procesată de Microsoft; aplicația nu primește datele tale de plată.

Политика конфиденциальности

Приложения не собирают, не передают и не продают персональные данные. Нет аккаунтов, аналитики и рекламных трекеров. Настройки, текст, история и статистика хранятся только на вашем устройстве. Покупка Pro в Microsoft Store обрабатывается Microsoft; приложение не получает ваши платёжные данные.
